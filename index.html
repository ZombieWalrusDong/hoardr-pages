HOARD.R

A personal listening log for Android

HOARD.R is an Android app that records the music you play, keeps the history on your own device, and lets you browse your most played artists, albums and tracks. It is a personal project by Matt Cornett and is not sold or offered as a service.

What it does

  • Captures what is playing on the device and saves each listen to a local database.
  • Shows your top artists, albums and tracks over different time windows.
  • Optionally sends listens to ListenBrainz or Last.fm accounts you connect yourself.
  • Optionally uploads summaries and a database backup to your own Google Drive, using Google sign-in.

Google sign-in

HOARD.R asks for one Google permission, access to files the app itself creates in your Google Drive. It cannot see your other Drive files. Details are in the privacy policy.
