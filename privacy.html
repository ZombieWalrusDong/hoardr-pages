Privacy policy

HOARD.R · last updated 10 November 2026

HOARD.R is a personal Android app by Matt Cornett. This page explains what data it handles.

Data the app keeps

The app stores your listening history (artist, album, track, time played) and your settings in a database on your own device. The developer has no server and does not receive this data.

Google sign-in and Drive

If you choose to sign in with Google, HOARD.R requests the drive.file permission. It lets the app create and read only the files it created in your Google Drive. The app uses it to upload library summaries and a backup of its database to your Drive when you tap Sync to Drive. The app does not read, change or delete any other file in your Drive. Your Google account name and email are not collected or stored by the developer. The sign-in token stays on your device, and you can remove access at any time in your Google Account under Security, Third-party access.

Other services you connect

If you connect ListenBrainz or Last.fm, the app sends your listens to that service under your account, and it looks up track information on MusicBrainz and similar music databases. Those services have their own privacy policies. If you connect none of them, nothing is sent.

What is not done

No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no sale or sharing of your data with anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its local database. Files the app uploaded to your Google Drive stay there until you delete them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. Questions go to the developer, Matt Cornett.
